  I am a Ph.D. candidate at [Bosch Research for Artificial                                              
  Intelligence](https://www.bosch.com/research/),                                                       
  Stuttgart, in cooperation with the [University of 
  Lübeck](https://www.inb.uni-luebeck.de/),                                                             
  advised by [Dr.-Ing. habil. Alexandru Paul Condurache](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=UTQb4jIAAAAJ).               
                                                                                         
  My research focuses on **3D scene understanding** and **LiDAR-based perception** for                  
  autonomous driving, with particular interest in self-supervised learning and foundation
  models for point clouds.
